References

Authoritative resources for students and educators to verify data, evaluate curriculum quality, and find trusted sources that support the Vision page.

B

Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS)

Official employment and occupational projections — use for workforce demand and long-term trend verification.

bls.gov
N

National Science Foundation (NSF)

Research publications and funding summaries relevant to STEM workforce and education.

nsf.gov
E

National Center for Education Statistics (NCES)

Student assessment datasets and NAEP reports for checking achievement and gaps.

nces.ed.gov
ED

U.S. Department of Education

Federal guidance, research summaries, and resources for K–12 policy and funding; useful for district-level program information and official guidance.

ed.gov
NG

Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)

Standards and performance expectations to align curriculum and assess quality.

nextgenscience.org
ER

ERIC / Education research

Use ERIC and Google Scholar to locate peer-reviewed studies that validate instructional methods and simulation outcomes.

eric.ed.gov
How to use these sources:
  • Check publication date and authorship; prefer recent, peer-reviewed work for research claims.
  • For statistics, cross-check claims against BLS/NCES tables rather than unsourced summaries.
  • Prefer classroom-tested simulations and cite original materials when using or adapting activities.
