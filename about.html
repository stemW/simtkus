About SimTk

SimTk (Simulation Technology) is a privacy-first, US-focused platform for sharing STEM educational content. Our goal is to empower educators, researchers, and students to collaborate and iterate on simulations and curriculum, using short reels and private sharing groups.

SimTk combines simple video reels, private groups, and mentorship tools to improve hands-on STEM learning.

For a concise, sourced statement of our vision and the data behind the need for a private STEM network, see the Vision page.
