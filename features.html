Features

High-impact features designed to attract investors, teachers, and students.

💡Highlights

  • Shareable Simulation Reels: short interactive simulations crafted for classroom use, demonstrations, and student projects.
  • Impact & Learning Signals: aggregated engagement and outcome metrics that help teachers refine lessons and schools measure learning progress.
  • Accessible & Privacy-First: private classroom groups, role-based controls, and streaming-first delivery to protect student data while enabling collaboration.

🏫For Teachers

  • Private Classroom Groups: share reels, assignments, and comment threads with student privacy controls.
  • Lesson Builder: drag-and-drop simulation steps that export lesson plans and standards-mapped activities.
  • Assessment & Insights: inline quizzes, rubric attachments, and classroom analytics to track progress.
  • Curriculum Versioning: fork, improve, and cite lesson versions for reproducibility.

🎓For Students

  • Short Guided Reels: snackable, interactive simulations with step-by-step hints and checkpoints.
  • Project Portfolios: students collect reels and notes into shareable portfolios for mentors and applications.
  • Mentor Matching: micro-mentorship sessions and office-hours booking integrated into groups.

🔧Core Platform

  • End-to-end privacy controls and role-based access (student, teacher, admin).
  • Secure media hosting with streaming endpoints (no direct download links exposed).
  • Extensible plugin API for assessments, analytics, and LMS integrations (LTI/Canvas).
  • Searchable library with tagging, standards mapping (NGSS), and peer review badges.

Roadmap & Quick Wins

  • Quarter 1: Private groups, upload + reels UX, and basic analytics.
  • Quarter 2: Lesson Builder, assessment blocks, and mentor matching MVP.
  • Quarter 3: Marketplace pilot, institutional reporting, and LTI integration.
